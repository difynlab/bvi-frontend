The BVI has a robust network of outstanding, experienced, and trusted experts based around the world. A number of BVI specialists and expert firms have developed to help service the needs of those looking to carry out cross-border trade and investment and want the comfort of the jurisdiction’s well-regarded company law.

These experts come from the world’s leading corporate firms, trust companies, law firms, and accounting firms, as well as others involved in the financial services sector.  They offer a sophisticated array of corporate services, transactions, litigation, as well as  wealth management solutions in trust and estate planning, funds and investment business, captive insurance and ship and aircraft registration services.

Our experts are supported by a mature regulatory infrastructure, a well-developed financial services industry and a flexible, creditor-friendly jurisdiction.
